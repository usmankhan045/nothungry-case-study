# NotHungry

## AI-Powered Nutrition Tracking App

NotHungry is a mobile nutrition tracking application built to make food logging easier and more convenient.

The app gives users different ways to record what they eat, including taking a photo of a meal, scanning a barcode, searching for food, and adding food manually. AI is used to help analyze meal photos and provide estimated nutrition information.

I built and shipped the mobile application as a client project.

---

## About the App

Food tracking can become repetitive when every meal has to be entered manually.

NotHungry is designed to reduce that effort.

A user can take a photo of a meal and get an estimated breakdown of the food and its nutrition. The result can then be reviewed and adjusted before it is added to the food diary.

The app also provides other ways to track food when a photo is not the right option.

### Main capabilities

- AI-assisted meal analysis
- Photo-based food logging
- Barcode scanning
- Food search
- Custom food entries
- Calorie tracking
- Macronutrient tracking
- Food diary
- Water tracking
- Supplement tracking
- Nutrition statistics
- Health platform integration

---

## How the Main Workflow Works

```text
Take a Photo
     |
     v
AI Analysis
     |
     v
Food & Nutrition Estimate
     |
     v
Review the Result
     |
     v
Make Adjustments
     |
     v
Save to Food Diary
     |
     v
Track Progress
```

The AI provides an estimate. The user can review and change the result before saving it.

---

## AI Meal Analysis

The main AI feature allows users to take a picture of their food and receive an estimated nutrition breakdown.

The workflow is designed to help identify:

- Food items
- Ingredients
- Estimated portions
- Calories
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Fat

Because food recognition and portion estimation are not exact, the app keeps the user involved in the process rather than treating the AI result as final without review.

---

## Other Food Logging Options

Not every meal needs to be logged through AI.

The application also supports:

### Barcode Scanning

Users can scan packaged food and use the available product information for tracking.

### Food Search

Users can search for food manually when they already know what they want to log.

### Custom Foods

Users can add their own food entries when a suitable option is not available.

---

## Nutrition Tracking

The app brings food records together in one place so users can keep track of their daily nutrition.

Depending on the available information, users can view:

- Daily calories
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Fat
- Water intake
- Meals
- Nutrition history
- Progress and statistics

---

## Food Diary

The food diary keeps a record of logged meals and makes it easier to look back at previous entries.

It provides a central place for users to review their food history and understand their nutrition over time.

---

## Water and Supplement Tracking

Nutrition tracking is not limited to meals.

The application also includes workflows for:

- Recording water intake
- Tracking supplements
- Reviewing daily records

These features are part of the wider nutrition tracking experience.

---

## Health Integration

The application supports health platform integration where available and where the user gives permission.

This allows supported health information to work alongside the user's nutrition tracking.

Health access is permission-based and remains under the user's control.

## Engineering Approach

One of the main challenges was making several different features work together as one application.

For example, food could enter the system through AI analysis, barcode scanning, manual search, or custom entries. These different paths still needed to produce a consistent experience for the user.

The same applies to nutrition history, water tracking, supplements, statistics, and health integrations.

The focus was not just on adding individual features. It was on making the complete experience work together smoothly.

---

## Product Principles

### Keep Food Logging Simple

The application should reduce unnecessary manual work.

### Keep the User in Control

AI helps with the process, but users can review and adjust their results.

### Make Information Easy to Understand

Nutrition information should be presented in a way that is useful during everyday use.

### Build for Real Users

Features need to work together reliably instead of existing only as isolated demonstrations.

### Respect User Permissions

Health and other device capabilities should only be accessed when the user gives permission.
